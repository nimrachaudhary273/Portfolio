case study 11

Aurelle

A luxury jewelry landing page for the Celeste Collection, built around large-format photography and quiet, editorial typography.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeE-commerce, luxury retail
ToolsHTML, CSS

The brief

Aurelle needed a landing page for its Celeste Collection that felt closer to a fashion editorial than a typical product page — the jewelry itself had to carry the page.

What I built

A full-bleed product photograph as the entire hero, a restrained serif headline ("Made of light. Kept for a lifetime.") centred directly over the piece, and a single outlined call to action so nothing competes with the photography.

Outcome

Placeholder — replace with a real result once you have one, once you can measure it (leads, bookings, engagement, or client feedback).
