Murray Family Reunion

An event microsite for an annual family reunion, built to keep relatives connected across events, family history, and shared memories.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeCommunity site
ToolsWordPress, CSS

The brief

The Murray family needed a simple, warm home base for their yearly reunion — something relatives could check for event details, family history, and member profiles without relying on a group chat that scrolls away.

What I built

A clean, content-first site organised around Home, Events, Legacy, Family Members, Location, and Contact — using real reunion photography as the emotional anchor and keeping navigation simple enough for relatives of every generation to use.
