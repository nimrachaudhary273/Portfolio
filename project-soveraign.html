case study 02

Soveraign Solutions Inc.

IT modernization and AI-readiness homepage — positioning a legacy-systems consultancy as a forward-looking transformation partner.

RoleFrontend Developer
Timeline4 weeks
TypeCorporate homepage

The brief

Soveraign wanted their homepage to signal readiness for AI-driven transformation work, while staying credible to enterprise IT buyers who are naturally skeptical of hype. The tone needed to be confident, not trendy.

What I built

A full homepage rebuild: a restrained hero with a portrait-style visual treatment, a services grid organized around client outcomes instead of internal department names, and a components library (buttons, cards, form fields) so the marketing and product teams could extend the site consistently after handoff.
