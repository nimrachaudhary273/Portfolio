case study 08

Gary G. Cochran

An author landing page for a thriller novelist, built around a striking full-bleed portrait and a reader email opt-in.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeAuthor / personal brand
ToolsHTML, CSS

The brief

Gary G. Cochran, author of the Jack Mercer Thriller Series, needed a landing page whose only job was to convert visitors into newsletter subscribers by offering a free prequel.

What I built

A single full-bleed author portrait, a New York Times pull-quote for instant credibility, a star rating, and one focused call to action — deliberately avoiding a crowded navigation bar that could distract from the opt-in.

Outcome
