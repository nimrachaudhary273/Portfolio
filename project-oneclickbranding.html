case study 07

One Click Branding

Two regional storefronts for the same printing and branding company — a bold, bilingual UAE build and a cleaner, conversion-focused US rebuild.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeE-commerce, multi-region
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS

The brief

One Click Branding operates in two very different markets. The UAE storefront needed to feel local and bilingual (Arabic and English) with a skyline-led visual identity, while the US storefront needed to prioritise speed and clarity for a more price-conscious, self-serve buyer.

What I built

Two distinct homepages sharing one product catalogue: the UAE build layers Arabic typography over a Dubai skyline hero, while the US rebuild strips that back to a plain-spoken headline, trust badges, and a direct "Shop Now" path.

Outcome
