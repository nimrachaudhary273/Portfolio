case study 01

SoverAlgn Solutions

Landing page hero for an enterprise AI platform — built to make a technical product feel immediately understandable to a non-technical buyer.

RoleFrontend Developer
Timeline3 weeks
TypeMarketing site

The brief

SoverAlgn needed a landing page that could explain an enterprise AI platform in under ten seconds of scroll, without leaning on jargon. The existing site buried the product's value under feature lists; the new hero needed to lead with outcomes.

What I built

A responsive hero section with a live-feeling animated data visual, a headline built around the customer's outcome rather than the technology, and a scroll-triggered reveal for supporting proof points. Every breakpoint from 320px to ultrawide was tested by hand, including reduced-motion and keyboard-navigation states.
