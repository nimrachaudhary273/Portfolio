QM Logics

A homepage redesign for QM Logics — moving from an illustration-led layout to a sharper, AI-forward visual language.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeRedesign / SaaS
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS

The brief

QM Logics' original homepage leaned on generic stock illustration and didn't clearly say what the company builds. As part of my day-to-day work here, the homepage needed a visual refresh that matched how far the product had come.

What I built

A redesign that replaced the illustrated hero with a sharper AI-and-code-forward visual, tightened the headline from a vague mission statement to a direct value proposition, and added trust metrics (years of experience, projects delivered, technologies) directly under the fold.
