case study 09

GilCom Media

A corporate homepage for a crisis-communications and public affairs firm, written and designed to project calm authority under pressure.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeCorporate / PR
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS

The brief

GilCom Media works with organisations in the middle of litigation, regulatory scrutiny, or a PR crisis. The homepage needed to read as capable and composed — the opposite tone of the crises their clients are facing.

What I built

A confident, editorial headline ("When Everything Is on the Line, You Need a Voice in the Room"), a documentary-style press-room image, and a prominent 24/7 crisis line alongside a lower-commitment consultation request.

Outcome
