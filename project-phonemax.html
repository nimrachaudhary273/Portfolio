case study 06

Phone Max

An e-commerce and booking site for a local phone repair shop, pairing a bold service hero with clear calls to call or request a quote.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeLocal business
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS

The brief

Phone Max needed a site that could handle two kinds of visitors — people wanting to browse phones for sale, and people who just cracked a screen and need a repair booked fast.

What I built

A hero built around real repair photography with a location-forward headline ("Your Tech Repair Specialist in Cheltenham"), a persistent call-now button, and dedicated navigation for Phones, Services, and Pick & Drop booking.
