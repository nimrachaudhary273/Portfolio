case study 04

WorldLink Team

A marketing site for a telecom reseller network, balancing a customer-facing cellular offer with a business-builder opportunity.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeMarketing site
ToolsHTML, CSS, JS

The brief

WorldLink Team needed one site to do two jobs at once: sell a cellular service plan to everyday customers, and pitch the same offer as a business opportunity to people looking for extra income.

What I built

A single-page layout with a clear savings-first headline, a supporting image carousel, and a wide navigation bar covering Advantages, Benefits, Program, and a built-in Shop — so both audiences can self-serve from the same homepage.
