case study 05

Femet

An industrial services homepage for a CNC metal machining and welding company, built to read as precise and technical at a glance.

RoleFrontend Developer
TypeIndustrial / B2B
ToolsHTML, CSS

The brief

Femet needed a homepage that could speak to procurement engineers and plant managers directly — no soft marketing language, just a clear statement of capability and a fast path to a quote.

What I built

A dark, machine-shop-toned hero built around real production photography, a direct headline, and two competing calls to action (quote request and direct email) placed above the fold, plus a language switcher for the company's Polish and English audiences.
